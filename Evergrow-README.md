Evergrow Packs
made with Sesame

Optional downloadable packs for Project: EVERGROW, a cozy, offline-first Android app that helps you build gentle daily habits and grows a little pixel garden as you go.

The app itself stays small. Extra skins, pets, decor and weather live here, and the app downloads only the ones you choose from inside the app.

What's here

- Skins: themed looks for the app and garden, like Starry, Farm, Cozy Cityscape and Rainy Café. Each one follows your phone's clock from dawn to night.
- Pets: animated garden visitors, like a cat and a puppy.
- Decor: benches, ponds, lanterns, fences and other garden items.
- Weather: rain, snow, fireflies and falling leaves.

The default garden and the three built-in skins (Day, Night and OLED) come with the app and don't need a download.

How packs are delivered

Each pack is a .zip file attached to a Release — ../../releases in this repo. The app reads the list of available packs, shows them in its shop, and downloads a pack only when you unlock it. Nothing is uploaded from your phone.

Credits and licenses

Packs may include art by other artists, used under their licenses. Every pack contains a CREDITS.txt with the artist, the source link and the license for each asset. Art made for Evergrow itself is credited the same way.

The art in these packs belongs to its artists. Please check each pack's CREDITS.txt before reusing anything outside Evergrow.
